I see each banner as a layered challenge. Choosing the right font, building a story, and shaping the layout—it all comes together through research, sketching, and intentional design. I often begin with drawn frames to set the composition, and from there, the visual rhythm starts to form.

My banners are interactive, driven by boldness and clarity. They’re not just designed to look good—they’re designed to speak.

Weinachy

→

wine e-commerce shop

Created for Weinarchy, an Austrian wine e-shop, this banner was designed for their appearance at a wine exhibition in Berlin, Germany. The concept was bold and immersive: one large poster split into five individual pieces, each standing as a visual fragment of a bigger story.

My goal was to reflect the brand’s confident, modern voice through dynamic layout and expressive typography—while ensuring each segment worked on its own and as part of the whole.

Font design

→

poster series

This is one of my favorite projects—a celebration of Georgian script through modern design. I created a custom display font by drawing bold, unique shapes for each letter, inspired by the rhythm and character of the Georgian alphabet.

Each letter then became the centerpiece of its own poster, transforming typography into powerful visual compositions. The result is a series that feels experimental, local, and deeply personal—bold in both form and cultural identity.

The Immortal Legacy

→

poster series

This personal project brings to life the statues of Georgia’s great historical figures through design. Combining authentic modern minimalism with subtle antique and bold elements, I reimagined these iconic sculptures as striking, contemporary visuals. This series is close to my heart—a fusion of heritage and fresh expression that celebrates Georgian culture in a new light.
