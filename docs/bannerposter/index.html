01 / Wine e-commerce

Weinachy

Posters & banners

Created for Weinachy, an Austrian wine e-shop, this banner was designed for their appearance at a wine exhibition in Berlin, Germany. The concept was bold and immersive: one large poster split into five individual pieces, each standing as a visual fragment of a bigger story. My goal was to reflect the brand’s confident, modern voice through dynamic layout and expressive typography—while ensuring each segment worked on its own and as part of the whole.

02 / NFT marketplace

Yoonico

Posters & banners

Commissioned from Italy, this series was designed for Yoonico, an innovative NFT e-shop where technology and business intersect. The goal was to reflect that futuristic energy with bold compositions and sleek typography. I created several animated and static banners that captured the brand’s forward-thinking spirit—clean, digital, and visually rich.

03 / Georgian typography

Font design

Posters & banners

This is one of my favorite projects—a celebration of Georgian script through modern design. I created a custom display font by drawing bold, unique shapes for each letter, inspired by the rhythm and character of the Georgian alphabet. Each letter then became the centerpiece of its own poster, transforming typography into powerful visual compositions. The result is a series that feels experimental, local, and deeply personal—bold in both form and cultural identity.

04 / Georgian cultural heritage

The Immortal Legacy

Posters & banners

This personal project brings to life the statues of Georgia’s great historical figures through design. Combining authentic modern minimalism with subtle antique and bold elements, I reimagined these iconic sculptures as striking, contemporary visuals. This series is close to my heart—a fusion of heritage and fresh expression that celebrates Georgian culture in a new light.
