01 / Family vodka from Guria

Gurian Vodka

Print design

This project was a deep visual exploration of a place and its spirit. Gurian Vodka is crafted using a traditional family recipe from Guria, and my task was to reflect that bold regional identity through design.

I drew inspiration from Guria’s vibrant nature, subtropical colors, wave-like geography, and even the forgotten Soviet mosaics of Lanchkhuti. These elements shaped the label’s composition and symbolism—especially the sun-sea motif and mosaic fragments, which I reinterpreted to give new life to disappearing local stories. The bottle’s form and proportions followed the mood of the label itself, resulting in a product that feels both culturally rooted and visually modern.

02 / Wine e-commerce

Weinachy

Print design

This project was a comprehensive dive into visual consistency across touchpoints. Working with Weinachy, an Austria-based company, I developed a wide range of brand materials to support both their corporate presence and public-facing communication.

From business cards, banners, and invoices to brochures, posters, and large-format Messe exposition graphics—I focused on creating a cohesive visual language that reflected the brand’s tone: professional, approachable, and distinctly European.

03 / Plant care

Growlab

Print design

Growlab is a modern plant care company focused on sustainable growing solutions. My collaboration with them centered around creating a warm, informative, and approachable visual language for their printed materials.

I designed a variety of assets—including guidebooks, brochures, booklets, business cards, sticker sets, and seed bunk labels—all aimed at supporting Growlab’s mission to make plant care simple and inspiring.

Each item was tailored to reflect their eco-conscious identity, using clean layouts, earthy tones, and playful but instructive graphics to engage both new and seasoned plant lovers.
