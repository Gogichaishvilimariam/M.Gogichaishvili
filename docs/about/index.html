Meet Mariam Gogichaishvili, a graphic designer who
has a passion for creating something out of nothing

I’m Mariam Gogichaishvili, a graphic designer who’s always had the passion to make something from nothing. I was around 13 or 14 when a local poet came to our school—some students read his work, and some of us made little videos.

I ended up making videos for all five teams. That was the first time I got curious about design software, and honestly, I’ve been hooked ever since.


Design & i

Currently, I specialize in branding, logo design, posters, motion graphics, simple 3D objects, custom fonts and forms. I even built my own portfolio website—and discovered I really enjoy web design too. I work both independently and with teams, and I’m most in my element when I’m deep in the creative process, chasing bold ideas and watching them come to life.
