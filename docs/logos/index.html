01 / Wine e-commerce

Weinachy

Logo design

The Weinachy logo was developed with a focus on clarity, structure, and distinctiveness. Drawing from the brand’s European roots, I crafted a minimal and modern mark that feels both professional and personal.

Clean lines, balanced proportions, and thoughtful spacing give the logo a confident visual presence, while subtle design choices ensure it stands apart with its own recognizable identity. It seamlessly adapts across digital and print applications, anchoring the brand’s visual language.

02 / NFT marketplace

Yoonico

Logo design

For Yoonico, a forward-thinking eCommerce and NFT marketplace, I created a bold and minimal logo that reflects innovation and digital clarity.

The design merges clean geometry with a tech-savvy aesthetic, capturing the brand’s dynamic nature while staying visually simple and adaptable. It’s built to perform across platforms—from product listings to blockchain interfaces—while maintaining a strong, recognizable identity in a fast-moving digital space.

03 / Music festival

Agora

Logo design

Agora Events, a vibrant music festival, needed a logo that radiated joy, rhythm, and movement. The challenge was to capture the energy of live music and human connection in a single mark.

While exploring visual directions, I came across a maze-like painting that instantly resonated with the spirit of the event. Inspired by its playful complexity, I developed a dynamic and cheerful logo that feels spontaneous yet intentional—just like a great festival experience.

04 / Water-inspired identity

Nami 8

Logo design

Nami 8 approached me with a unique challenge: to create a 3D logo that would embody the essence of a single drop of water.

Inspired by the fluidity and form of water itself, I designed a custom typeface built entirely from drop-like shapes. The result is a soft, flowing, and sculptural logo that feels organic yet refined—perfectly aligned with the brand’s name and concept.
